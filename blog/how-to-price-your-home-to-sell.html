How to Price Your Home to Sell

By Rob Vettese · November 26, 2026 · 3 min read

Of all the decisions you'll make when selling, the list price has the biggest effect on how quickly your home sells and what you walk away with. Here's how I approach it with sellers.

Start with Comparable Sales

A comparative market analysis looks at homes similar to yours that have recently sold nearby. I look at:

  • Sold prices, not asking prices. What buyers actually paid is what matters.
  • Size, layout and lot. Square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, parking and lot size.
  • Condition and updates. A renovated kitchen or new roof changes value.
  • Timing. A sale from a year ago may not reflect today's market.

I also look at homes that are currently for sale, since those are your competition, and homes that didn't sell, which often show where buyers drew the line.

Understand the Current Market

The same house can be priced differently depending on conditions. When there are fewer homes for sale and lots of buyers, homes tend to sell faster. When there's more inventory, buyers have more choice and take more time. I'll share the current local numbers for your area so you can set realistic expectations.

Common Pricing Mistakes

  • Pricing too high "to leave room." Overpriced homes often sit, and a home that's been on the market a long time can attract lower offers.
  • Pricing based on what you need. Your mortgage balance or renovation costs don't change what buyers will pay.
  • Relying on online estimates alone. Automated estimates can't see your home's condition or features.

Prepare the Home to Match the Price

Small fixes, a deep clean and good photos help buyers see value. Before listing, we'll walk through the home and decide what's worth doing and what isn't. If you need help, I can connect you with contractors I trust.

Review Offers on More Than Price

When offers come in, the highest number isn't always the best offer. Conditions, deposit size and closing date matter too. We'll go through each one together.
