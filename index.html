About me

A trusted advisor who knows Niagara well

Buying, selling, or investing in property is a major step, and you deserve a trusted advisor who knows the Niagara Region well. With 7+ years of experience, I’ve guided clients through a wide range of transactions, from first-time homebuyers to luxury property sales, and I take the time to explain every step so you feel confident in your decisions.

Step by step

What to expect, start to finish

01

Talk about your goals

Budget, must-haves, neighbourhoods and timing. We figure out what really matters to you.

02

Get pre-approved

Speak with a lender or mortgage broker so you know your price range before you start touring.

03

Search & tour

I'll send you homes that fit, book showings, and give you my honest take on each one.

04

Offer & negotiate

We review comparable sales together, decide on price and conditions, and I negotiate for you.

05

Conditions to closing

Inspection, financing and paperwork. I keep everything on track and keep you informed until you get the keys.

01

Pricing consultation

I'll walk you through recent nearby sales and give you a realistic price range for today's market.

02

Get market-ready

We decide what's worth fixing. If you need trades, I can connect you with contractors I trust.

03

List & show

Your home goes on MLS®, and I manage showings and share feedback with you along the way.

04

Review offers

We go over every offer together, terms as well as price, and I negotiate to protect your interests.

05

Sold to closed

I handle the details through conditions and closing and keep you updated at every step.

Every move is different. These steps give you a feel for the process, and we'll tailor it to your situation.

Investments & Construction

Part of the Investment Team

I work as part of an investment team, so you get more than a standard real estate service. When you're looking at an income property or a project, you have people around the table who analyze deals, renovate, build and manage property. Every decision starts with the numbers and a clear plan.

“Every single tenant paid every months rent on time…” — Jim Ginwalla, investment property owner

Explore investingTalk to Rob
01

Investments

Finding, running the numbers on, and growing income properties in Niagara.

  • Deal analysis using realistic rents, expenses and cash flow
  • Single-family rentals, duplexes and multi-unit properties
  • Value-add opportunities, including secondary suite potential
  • Portfolio planning: when to buy, hold, refinance or sell
  • Leasing support, tenant screening and placement
02

Construction

Seeing what a property could be, and what it will realistically take to get there.

  • Renovation planning and budgeting before you buy
  • Additional dwelling units, basement suites and conversions
  • New build and infill opportunities
  • Assessing condition, upgrades and hidden costs
  • Connections to trusted trades and contractors
