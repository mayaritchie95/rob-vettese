Step by step

What to expect, start to finish

01

Talk about your goals

Budget, must-haves, neighbourhoods and timing. We figure out what really matters to you.

02

Get pre-approved

Speak with a lender or mortgage broker so you know your price range before you start touring.

03

Search & tour

I'll send you homes that fit, book showings, and give you my honest take on each one.

04

Offer & negotiate

We review comparable sales together, decide on price and conditions, and I negotiate for you.

05

Conditions to closing

Inspection, financing and paperwork. I keep everything on track and keep you informed until you get the keys.

01

Pricing consultation

I'll walk you through recent nearby sales and give you a realistic price range for today's market.

02

Get market-ready

We decide what's worth fixing. If you need trades, I can connect you with contractors I trust.

03

List & show

Your home goes on MLS®, and I manage showings and share feedback with you along the way.

04

Review offers

We go over every offer together, terms as well as price, and I negotiate to protect your interests.

05

Sold to closed

I handle the details through conditions and closing and keep you updated at every step.

Every move is different. These steps give you a feel for the process, and we'll tailor it to your situation.
