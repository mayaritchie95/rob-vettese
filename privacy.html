Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

This policy explains how Rob Vettese and RE/MAX Niagara Realty Ltd., Brokerage collect, use, share and protect personal information through this website and when you contact us. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

1. Who We Are

This website is operated by Rob Vettese, a real estate agent registered with RE/MAX Niagara Realty Ltd., Brokerage. When this policy says "we", "us" or "our", it means Rob Vettese and RE/MAX Niagara Realty Ltd., Brokerage. The brokerage is responsible for personal information collected in the course of its real estate business and may have its own privacy policy, which also applies.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Give Us

  • Your name, email address and phone number when you fill out a form, email, call or text.
  • Anything you include in a message, such as the type of home you're looking for, your timeline, or a property address for a home evaluation.
  • Your choice about whether to receive marketing emails, and the date and way you gave it.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Like most websites, our hosting provider may record basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, for security and to keep the site running.
  • This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google. See Google's privacy policy for how it handles this information.
  • We do not currently use advertising cookies or analytics tracking. If we add analytics (for example, Google Analytics) or advertising pixels, we'll update this policy first.

The mortgage calculator runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter are not sent to us or stored.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your questions and requests, such as a home evaluation, sold-price information or listing alerts you've asked for.
  • To provide real estate services if you choose to work with us.
  • To send you marketing emails, only if you've agreed to receive them (see section 6).
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations, including those under the Trust in Real Estate Services Act, 2002 (TRESA) and federal anti-money-laundering rules (FINTRAC).
  • To keep this website secure and working properly.

We will not use your information for a new purpose without telling you and, where required, asking for your consent.

4. When We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only when needed:

  • With RE/MAX Niagara Realty Ltd., Brokerage, which supervises its agents and keeps transaction records.
  • With service providers who help us run our business, such as website hosting, email, form and customer-relationship tools. They may only use your information to provide services to us.
  • With others involved in your transaction, such as other brokerages, lawyers, lenders, appraisers or inspectors, when you work with us and it is needed to complete your transaction.
  • When required by law, for example to respond to a regulator such as the Real Estate Council of Ontario (RECO), FINTRAC, or a court order.

Some of our service providers may store information outside Canada, where it may be subject to the laws of that country.

5. How Long We Keep It

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires. Records related to a real estate transaction are kept for the periods required by law (for example, FINTRAC requires certain records to be kept for at least five years). General inquiries that don't lead to a transaction are deleted when no longer needed.

6. Marketing Emails (CASL)

We only send marketing emails, such as listing alerts and market updates, if you've given express consent (for example, by checking the optional box on our contact form), or where the law otherwise permits. Every marketing email will identify us, include our contact information, and include a working unsubscribe link. You can unsubscribe at any time, and we'll process your request within 10 business days.

7. How We Protect It

We use reasonable physical, administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal information from loss, theft and unauthorized access. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so please don't send sensitive information (such as banking details or ID numbers) through our website forms.

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual limits. Withdrawing consent may affect our ability to provide some services.
  • You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm your identity before responding.

9. Children

This website is not intended for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
